Signal labels (Supportive, Mixed, Headwind) come from simple rules written for this dashboard and stated on each card. They are not Druckenmiller's own thresholds. "Supportive" means the reading looks like his January setup (loose policy, breakouts, broad participation, tight spreads); "Headwind" means it looks like his November one.

Data: FRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis), including ICE BofA spread series; Yahoo Finance via yfinance. Refreshed each weekday evening by GitHub Actions.
